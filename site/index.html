For Mac · macOS 15 or later

Character Sheets

Now in open beta on TestFlight: join the beta with this link.

A light weight native mac app for OSR D&D. Currently using the OSE rules. It saves your characters as human reable plain text, easy to back up and share. I've tried to provide a simple, clean interface that doesn't get in the way of your game.

Old-School Essentials

Requires Old-School Essentials. The app knows the Classic Fantasy classes' numbers (saves, attacks, experience, spells per day) and leaves the rules themselves to the books.

Support

Questions, bug reports and ideas are welcome. In the app, choose Help → Send Feedback…, or email soggybag@gmail.com.

Privacy

The app collects no personal information and doesn't require an internet connection. Read the privacy policy.
