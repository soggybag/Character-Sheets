New in this build:

- Buying equipment: under Equipment, Add from List… shows the rules' weapons, armour, ammunition and adventuring gear with costs. Add an item and it's paid for from the character's money, with change in gold, silver and copper. Untick Pay for something found or given. Items your class can't normally use are marked but can still be added.
- New Character has the same list: roll your starting gold, then Buy Equipment… to kit out the character before creating them. AC is filled in from the armour you buy.
- Weapons on the sheet show their rules damage in grey.

Things to try:
- Kit out a new character from scratch.
- Go shopping between adventures with an existing character: does the money come out right?
