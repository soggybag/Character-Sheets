## Description (4,000 max)

> Character Sheets is a lightweight Mac app for Old-School Essentials
> characters. It fills in the numbers the rules decide, stays out of the way
> of the ones you decide, and keeps every character in a plain text file you
> own.
>
> REQUIRES OLD-SCHOOL ESSENTIALS
> The app knows the Classic Fantasy classes' numbers: saving throws,
> attacks, experience, hit dice, spells per day and thief skills. The rules
> themselves are in the books.
>
> ROLL UP A CHARACTER
> • Roll 3d6 in order, pick a class and see which ones your scores qualify
>   for, adjust scores for a better XP bonus, roll hit points and gold, and
>   buy equipment.
> • Or roll a whole character in one click, then change anything you like.
> • Every die is shown, and you can type in rolls made with real dice.
> • House rules if your table uses them: 4d6 drop the lowest, and swapping
>   scores.
>
> A SHEET THAT KNOWS THE RULES
> • Saves, THAC0 or ascending attack bonus, AC, movement, exploration and
>   thief skills are filled in from the class, level, scores and gear.
> • Your own values always win: a magic item or a ruling is kept, marked
>   with a small dot, and never changed behind your back.
> • Level up shows every change before it happens and rolls the new hit die.
>   Level down handles energy drain.
> • Gentle notes point out things worth knowing, like a class restriction or
>   an encumbered character. Nothing is ever blocked.
>
> AT THE TABLE
> • Damage and healing in one click, spells marked as cast, and "use one"
>   for arrows and torches.
> • Combat movement per round, exploration per turn, and overland travel per
>   day, with encumbrance.
> • Keep the party side by side or in tabs, or open a party folder to see
>   everyone's HP, AC, attacks and saves at a glance.
> • Make the text bigger for reading across the table.
> • Print or export a one-page PDF sheet.
>
> YOUR FILES, YOUR WAY
> • Each character is a Markdown file with a short header. Read it in any
>   text editor, keep it in iCloud Drive or Dropbox, put it under version
>   control.
> • A custom equipment list lets your group change prices, drop items the
>   market doesn't sell, and add house items.
> • Import filled-in PDF character sheets.
> • No account, no internet connection, no data collected.
>
> Character Sheets is an independent app. It isn't an official Old-School
> Essentials product, and isn't affiliated with or approved by Necrotic
> Gnome.
>
> Old-School Essentials is a trademark of Necrotic Gnome. The trademark and
> Old-School Essentials logo are used with permission of Necrotic Gnome,
> under license. Game rules data is Open Game Content used under the Open
> Game License 1.0a.
