## Description (4,000 max)

> 3d6 is a lightweight Mac app for Old-School Essentials characters. It fills in the numbers the rules decide, stays out of the way of the ones you decide, and keeps every character in a plain text file you own.
>
> REQUIRES OLD-SCHOOL ESSENTIALS
> The app knows the numbers for the Classic Fantasy and Advanced Fantasy classes and races: saving throws, attacks, experience, hit dice, spells per day and skills. The rules themselves are in the books.
>
> ROLL UP A CHARACTER
> • Roll 3d6 in order, pick a class and see which ones your scores qualify for, adjust scores for a better XP bonus, roll hit points and gold, and buy equipment.
> • Or roll a whole character in one click, then change anything you like.
> • Every die is shown, and you can type in rolls made with real dice.
> • House rules if your table uses them: 4d6 drop the lowest, and swapping scores.
>
> CLASSIC AND ADVANCED FANTASY
> • Every Classic Fantasy class, plus Advanced Fantasy's acrobat, assassin, barbarian, bard, druid, illusionist, knight, paladin and ranger, and its drow, duergar, gnome, half-elf, half-orc and svirfneblin.
> • Play race-as-class, or choose race and class separately: a dwarf thief, an elf ranger. The race's modifiers, class choices and level limits are shown as you go.
> • Each class's own skills on the sheet: a thief's, an acrobat's tightrope walking, an assassin's assassination, a ranger's tracking.
>
> A SHEET THAT KNOWS THE RULES
> • Saves, THAC0 or ascending attack bonus, AC, movement, exploration and skills are filled in from the class, race, level, scores and gear.
> • Class and race abilities at the character's level, with the ones still to come.
> • Your own values always win: a magic item or a ruling is kept, marked with a small dot, and never changed behind your back.
> • Level up shows every change before it happens and rolls the new hit die. Level down handles energy drain.
> • Gentle notes point out things worth knowing, like a class restriction, a race's level limit or an encumbered character. Nothing is ever blocked.
>
> AT THE TABLE
> • Damage and healing in one click, spells marked as cast, and "use one" for arrows and torches.
> • Combat movement per round, exploration per turn, and overland travel per day, with encumbrance.
> • Keep the party side by side or in tabs, or open a party folder to see everyone's HP, AC, attacks and saves at a glance.
> • Make the text bigger for reading across the table.
> • Print or export a one-page PDF sheet.
>
> YOUR FILES, YOUR WAY
> • Each character is a Markdown file with a short header. Read it in any text editor, keep it in iCloud Drive or Dropbox, put it under version control.
> • A custom equipment list lets your group change prices, drop items the market doesn't sell, and add house items.
> • Import filled-in PDF character sheets.
> • No account, no internet connection, no data collected.
>
> 3d6 is an independent app. It isn't an official Old-School Essentials product, and isn't affiliated with or approved by Necrotic Gnome.
>

Game rules data is Open Game Content used under the Open Game License 1.0a. Permission to include details of non-SRD races and classes granted by Necrotic Gnome.
